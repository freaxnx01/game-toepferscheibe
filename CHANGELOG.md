# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here, following
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com) and
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Nasser Glanz: Der Ton glänzt beim Formen stellenweise nass und trocknet in einigen Sekunden ab (#7).
- Galerie: Werke lassen sich in 3D anschauen und drehen (#13).
- Speicherschutz: Der Browser wird um dauerhaften Speicher gebeten, und auf iPad/iPhone erscheint einmalig ein Tipp zum Home-Bildschirm (#5).

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-23

### Added

- Self-hosted Umami analytics (page views and a `game_start` event), with a privacy section and a „Datenschutz“ link on the start screen (#12).
- Tool mode with rim notches and corner shapes (#2); at most 16 notches, with a hint when one is refused (#9).
- Gallery works can be reopened with „Weiter töpfern“, and the unsaved piece is reachable from the gallery (#1).
- Soft motor hum and clay rub sound, off by default (#3).
- Choice of the starting clay lump: size and form (#4).
- All firing steps are shown, advanced with a button (#10).
- 12 glaze colours and a „Zufällige Form“ button.
- Favicon.

### Changed

- Clay is heavier: it flows sluggishly towards the finger instead of jumping (#6).
- Clay has a uniform, clay-coloured look without grain.
- Gallery works and the kiln no longer show the turntable (#11).
